# Genre & Mood Knowledge Base

Short reference notes the RAG pipeline retrieves and hands to Claude so its
explanations are grounded in what each label means in this catalog.
Each `## <label>` heading is one retrievable entry (genre or mood).

## pop

Polished, hook-driven songs built for wide appeal. Usually mid-to-high energy, danceable, and mostly electronic production.

## lofi

Low-fidelity hip-hop-influenced beats with soft drums and warm, dusty textures. Low energy and good for studying or background focus.

## rock

Guitar-driven band music with live drums. In this catalog it leans loud and driving, with high energy and low acousticness.

## ambient

Atmospheric, slow-moving soundscapes with little or no beat. Very low energy, often used for relaxation or sleep.

## jazz

Improvised, swing- or groove-based music with acoustic instruments like piano, upright bass and horns. Relaxed coffee-shop energy.

## synthwave

Retro 1980s-inspired synth music with pulsing basslines. Moody, nocturnal, medium-high energy and electronic.

## indie pop

Pop made with a lighter, independent-label feel: jangly guitars, bright melodies, slightly more organic than mainstream pop.

## hip hop

Rhythm-and-rhyme music built on beats and samples. Confident delivery, strong groove, high danceability.

## classical

Orchestral or solo instrumental art music. Highly acoustic and often low energy; can be deeply emotional.

## edm

Electronic dance music made for clubs and festivals: big drops, four-on-the-floor kicks, very high energy.

## country

Storytelling songs rooted in American folk, with acoustic guitar, fiddle and steel guitar. Warm and often nostalgic.

## metal

Heavy, distorted guitars, aggressive drums and intense vocals. The highest-energy, least acoustic genre here.

## happy

Upbeat, positive songs with high valence — good for celebrating, driving with friends, or lifting a mood.

## chill

Calm and easygoing without being sad. Good for studying, reading, or winding down.

## intense

Driving, high-adrenaline music for workouts, gaming or anything that needs a push.

## relaxed

Laid-back and comfortable, a little warmer and more social than "chill" — think Sunday morning coffee.

## moody

Atmospheric and a bit brooding; night drives and introspective listening.

## focused

Steady, unobtrusive music that keeps you in the zone without demanding attention.

## confident

Swagger and self-assurance; strong rhythms that make you feel in charge.

## melancholic

Wistful, sad or reflective; music for processing emotions or quiet evenings.

## euphoric

Peak-moment joy and release — festival highs and big emotional lifts.

## nostalgic

Evokes memories and simpler times; warm, bittersweet storytelling.

## angry

Aggressive, cathartic energy for venting frustration.
